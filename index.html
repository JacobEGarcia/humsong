Humsuite · 01 · Voice as seed

HUMSONG

Hum a melody for a few seconds. The machine transcribes it, then keeps writing — new sections, a hook made of your own phrase, an ending. Your melody, continued long after you stop.

Allow the microphone, then hum one clear melody line. 5–20 seconds is plenty.
Transcription
Song

Everything runs in this page. Your hum is pitch-tracked, quantised and continued by a small composition engine on your own device — nothing is uploaded. Inspired by the YuE2 hum-to-song adapter released by The Fixed Seed Company (hum in, song out). Non-commercial, just like their weights.
